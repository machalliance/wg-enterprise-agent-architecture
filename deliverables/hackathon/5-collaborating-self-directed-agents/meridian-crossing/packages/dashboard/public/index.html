Proof each deal really happened

The buyer and each supplier keep their own signed record of what they agreed — there is no shared database either side could quietly edit, and neither ever reads the other's log. Instead each side independently works out a transaction record from the messages it already holds and publishes one fingerprint of it. Identical fingerprints are cryptographic proof the deal is real and neither side can deny it.

Stripe settlement

When a deal commits, the buyer opens a Stripe crypto PaymentIntent for the full amount. Stripe issues a USDC deposit address on the Tempo network; the buyer agent sends USDC to it, and Stripe watches the chain and captures the payment automatically once the funds settle. A deal over the auto-pay limit waits for a human to approve opening the payment.
